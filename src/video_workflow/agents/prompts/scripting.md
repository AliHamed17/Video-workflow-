Constraints
- Total length about $target_duration seconds; each shot is exactly $shot_duration seconds,
  so use $shot_count shots (index from 0).
- Voiceover is spoken narration: at most about $words_per_shot words per shot so it fits.
  On-screen text is a separate short headline — at most ~6 words per shot.

Structure that holds attention (label each shot's beat)
1. hook — shot 0: a striking image plus a line that opens a question in under 2 seconds.
   Tease the payoff; do not reveal or show it yet.
2. setup / escalation — every middle shot adds one new fact or raises the stakes. No recap,
   no repeated visuals.
3. twist — around the middle, a re-hook that resets attention ("But that's not the strangest part…").
4. payoff — the most surprising fact or image, in the final third.
5. loop — the last line flows back into the first line so the video loops cleanly.

Write for the ear
- Short sentences, active verbs, "you". One idea per shot.
- Concrete comparisons to everyday things ("as heavy as a car", "faster than a blink") and real
  numbers beat adjectives. No filler ("basically", "in fact", "did you know", "let's dive in").
- emphasis: 1–2 words copied exactly from that shot's voiceover that carry the punch (a number,
  the surprising word). The captions light them up, so pick sparingly.

Write for the camera
- visual: what an AI video model can show in ONE continuous $shot_duration-second take — one
  main subject, one clear action, a real place. Each shot looks different from the last (new
  scale, angle or place). No text or numbers on screen, no crowds, no famous people, brands or
  characters, no fine hand work.

Accuracy
- Only state what is well established. Prefer "up to", "about", "scientists think" over
  false precision. The visual of each shot must show what the narration says at that moment
  (correct order of events, correct scale).
