- visual_mechanic: the visual idea that makes it addictive (the thing AI video does best).

  characters, our own script and look. Never reuse the original's script, characters, names,
  logos, music or exact shots; we borrow the format, not the content.
- our_version: a complete idea (title, hook, angle, pillar, why_it_will_perform,
  fact_check_notes, predicted_score). If it states facts, they must be well established.
