What a cast is for
- Character-led AI shorts (an animal host who explains, a cast that reacts, a mini-series) hold
  attention far better than faceless footage: people follow characters. The host appears in every
  video and talks to the viewer; the others recur when the topic fits.
- Each character must be ORIGINAL (no existing cartoon, mascot, celebrity or brand character, nothing
  that resembles one), instantly readable on a phone screen, and different from the others in
  silhouette, colour and voice.
- They serve the channel's content: a host who can credibly explain the niche, with a clear
  personality and a short catchphrase (at most once a video).

For each character
- look: the visual bible an artist could draw identically every time — shape and proportions, named
  colours, eyes, outfit and accessories, and ONE distinctive feature. No text or logos on clothes.
- being: human, humanoid (an anthropomorphic animal on two legs) or creature (an animal on its own
  body). AI video keeps humanoids and creatures most consistent.
- voice and voice_gender: how they sound (age, pitch, energy).

Pick one style for the whole cast (Classic 3D = feature-animation 3D; Painterly 3D; CGI; Noir 3D;
Anime; 2.5D; Furry; Classic 2D; Painterly 2D; Realism) and pin it with style_notes. Say in `format`
how the cast appears in every video, and in `why` why this cast fits this audience.
